# Prompt Contract

The pre-read pipeline is not trying to reproduce every highlight.

It should optimize for:

- red-style importance first: key facts, structure, stakes, and mechanisms
- high-signal yellow highlights second
- enough chronological and institutional orientation to make the real read faster
- short, readable prose that can be scanned before opening the book

It should not optimize for:

- blue/personal-resonance highlights
- exhaustive chunk notes
- generic mental-model labels that hide the event
- a separate "questions to verify" section unless the book itself makes a central doubtful claim
- long summaries that feel like homework before the book

A generated pre-read brief is correct only if:

- the reader can explain the main arc before reading
- the reader knows which people, factions, institutions, and constraints to watch
- the reader sees the main power moves and causal mechanisms
- the reader is not buried in non-load-bearing detail
- the prose is good enough to actually read
