# Review Checklist

Use this after a real `scripts/preread_book.py` run.

Mark each item pass/fail:

- The brief is short enough to read before the book.
- The first section orients me to time period, factions, stakes, and central problem.
- The arc is chronological enough that I will not get lost while reading.
- The brief captures the most important red-style facts and mechanisms.
- The brief captures enough high-signal yellow material to make the book easier to read.
- It does not over-index on personal/blue material.
- It does not drown me in names, episode lists, or trivia.
- It does not contain generic framework prose where a concrete event should be.
- It does not create a useless "questions to verify" section.
- The prose is something I would willingly read before opening the book.

Specific Master of the Senate checks:

- Does it explain that Johnson staged visible floor drama because the appearance of power compounded real power?
- Does it explain the Rowe recruitment scene as pity/social pressure followed by immediate hierarchy reset?
- Does it distinguish Johnson's real legislative skill from mere colorful intimidation?
- Does it preserve the civil-rights arc without implying Johnson was simply liberal or simply cynical?
